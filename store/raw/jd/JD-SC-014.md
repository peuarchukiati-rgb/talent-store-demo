# Demand & Supply Planning Lead — Dong Nai factory

> SYNTHETIC JOB DESCRIPTION. Written for a demo. Not a real Nestlé opening.

## The role

Own the monthly planning cycle for the Dong Nai factory: turn the demand plan into a
feasible supply plan, keep inventory inside policy, and bring the factory and
commercial teams to one number every month.

## What you will do

- Run demand review and supply review, and prepare the monthly sign-off pack.
- Keep planning master data in SAP clean (lead times, safety stock, shelf life).
- Coach two planners and one junior analyst.
- Work with the regional planning team in English and with the factory floor in Vietnamese.

Listed in the frontmatter as R1–R5 (must) and N1–N3 (nice to have).
